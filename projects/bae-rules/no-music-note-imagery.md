## No music-note imagery

bae never shows a music note. That covers SF Symbols (`music.note`,
`music.note.list`, `music.note.house`, …), Material icons (`MusicNote`,
`LibraryMusic`, `QueueMusic`, …), the ♪ ♫ ♬ characters and their emoji, and any
drawing of a note in an app icon, placeholder, illustration, website page or
doc.

A music library doesn't need a note to say it holds music. Where one stood in
for something real, show the real thing: the album's cover, or a plain tile
where there is none; a word where a control needs a label.
