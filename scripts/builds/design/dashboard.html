This board draws the page as built: the title and figures (load, free disk, each product repo's commit hook times), the Needs attention box, each repo's group with its app build lanes, its worktree cards and its +N free worktrees tile, and the Lists panel at the right. Each app checkout is a lane card: green all over while it builds, with who builds, the programs, CPU and memory, and how many builds wait in the lane's queue, which opens to reorder or take out a build. A lane's checkouts share one queue, so each Mac lane card counts the same waiting builds. Each worktree card has a band with its state (Waiting on you, Ready to land or Working) and its disk, its name and branch, its items' titles, one plain line of what it waits on or does, the commits waiting to land and a footer of times and tree state. The cards sort by state in that order. A worktree whose branch waits for an app build is Working, and its line gives its place in the queue.
