**No marketing language anywhere.** Applies to written artifacts where the goal
is explanation or implementation: code, comments, docs, notes, app UI text,
error messages, commit messages, PR descriptions, and conversational
explanations to the user. No "improves user experience," "seamless," "powerful,"
"elegant," "robust," "intuitive," "delightful," etc. State what it does plainly.

Public non-doc website pages are exempt, including landing pages, pricing pages,
feature pages, download pages, and their localized copy. Docs pages are still
covered, even when they live under the website app.

*(Pairs with "No pitch-deck framing" in `instructions-agent.md` — that rule
covers strategy/business vocabulary specifically.)*
